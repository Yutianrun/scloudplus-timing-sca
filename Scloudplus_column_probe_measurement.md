# Sparse C1 column probe measurement

Source: latest downloaded Scloud+ implementation in `latest-download/`; level 256, SHAKE.

Probe construction:

- `C1[0][0] = a`, all other C1 coefficients zero;
- `C2` is the encoding of a fixed baseline message (the test program searches a small baseline/offset set);
- `a` is scanned over 0..1023 for one generated key;
- the recovered message is obtained with the original `pke_dec`, then its FO sampler profile is computed with the fixed public key;
- low/high probes are selected from the scan and measured with the uninstrumented original decapsulation.

For this run, the selected probes differed in total sampler refill rounds but both had the same SHAKE generated-byte bucket (29,376 bytes). Consequently the full decapsulation timing difference was not statistically clear.

| build | calls per probe | mean high-low | block SD | approximate 95% CI for mean |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| reference | 2,000 | +0.649 us | 18.011 us | +/-3.548 us |
| NEON | 2,000 | +0.037 us | 12.189 us | +/-2.401 us |

The local sampler-only differences were also not significant for this pair. This does not disprove a timing channel; it means this sparse probe and selected key did not create a different XOF refill bucket. A stronger next search should enumerate candidate secret vectors and scan C2 baselines/offsets for profiles that differ in generated bytes or a repeatable timing class.
